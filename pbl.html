TamilED - PBL - Progressive Based Joyful Learning

What Do We Want to Accomplish?

  • Carry Tamil forward to future generations
    • Foster a genuine love and appreciation for the Tamil language.
    • Empower today's learners to pass Tamil on to their own future generations.
  • Use an effective, modern approach
    • Engage students with fun, practical learning activities.
    • Keep the curriculum learner-centered.
    • Give teachers the autonomy to adapt to their students' needs.

How Do We Accomplish This?

  • Level 1 - Beginner - Play Based Learning
    • Play interactive games in Tamil.
    • Enjoy stories and songs in Tamil
  • Level 2 - Intermediate - Project Based Learning
    • Build hands-on creations through arts, crafts, and creative projects.
    • Practice reading, writing, and presenting in Tamil.
  • Level 3 - Advanced - Problem Based Learning
    • Tackle and solve real-life problems.
    • Research, analyze, create, and deliver solutions entirely in Tamil.

Will This Work?

Consider the difference between textbook study and natural immersion:
  • Learning English in school: I studied English in school every day for over 10 years, taught by a teacher using a well-designed curriculum. Even though I scored really well on tests, I couldn't even speak one or two sentences properly.
  • Learning Telugu from playing: I had the opportunity to play with Telugu-speaking kids from a neighboring village. Even though we only played once a week—and it was tough at first—I could understand Telugu within six months and was speaking it within a year. Later, I learned to read and write on my own out of pure interest.
This proves that kids can acquire a great deal of Tamil naturally through fun-filled activities.
