TamilED - PBL - Progressive Based Joyful Learning

What do we want to accomplish?

  • Take Tamil to next generations
    • Instill love for Tamil in them
    • Equip them to take Tamil to their next generations
  • Using the right approach
    • Fun and practical learning activities
    • Learner centered
    • Teacher autonomy

How do we plan to accomplish this?

  • Leave 1 - Beginner - Play Based Learning
    • Play games in Tamil
    • Enjoy stories and songs in Tamil
  • Level 2 - Intermediate - Project Based Learning
    • Create things like arts, crafts, etc
    • Read, write and present
  • Level 3 - Advanced - Problem Based Learning
    • Solve real life problems
    • Study, analyze, create and deliver in Tamil

Will this work?

  • Learning English from school
    • I studied English in school every day for over 10 years, taught by a teacher using a well-designed curriculum. Even though I scored really well on tests, I couldn't even speak one or two sentences properly.
  • Learning Telugu from playing
    • I had the opportunity to play with Telugu-speaking kids from a neighboring village. Even though we only played once a week—and it was tough at first—I could understand Telugu within six months and was speaking it within a year. Later, I learned to read and write on my own out of pure interest.
This proves that kids can acquire a great deal of Tamil naturally through fun-filled activities.
