TamilED - PBL - Progressive Based Learning

What do we want to accomplish?

  • Take Tamil to next generations
    • Instill love for Tamil in them
    • Equip them to take Tamil to their next generations
  • Using the right approach
    • Fun and practical learning activities
    • Learner centered
    • Teacher autonomy

How do we plan to accomplish this?

  • Leave 1 - Beginner - Play Based Learning
    • Play games in Tamil
    • Enjoy stories and songs in Tamil
  • Level 2 - Intermediate - Project Based Learning
    • Create things like arts, crafts, etc
    • Read, write and present
  • Level 3 - Advanced - Problem Based Learning
    • Solve real life problems
    • Study, analyze, create and deliver in Tamil

Will this work?

  • Learning English from school
    • I learned English in school for 10+ years on a daily basis from a teacher using a well designed curriculum. I would score really well in tests, but could not speak even one or two sentences properly.
  • Learning Telugu from playing
    • I had opportunity to play with Telugu kids from a neighboring village. While it was hard initially I was able to comprehend Telugu in about 6 months and was about to respond in about a year. This was possible only playing once a week with those kids. Later I was able to develop literacy skill out of my own interest. So, it is possible to acquire a lot of Tamil with fun filled activities.
